PRIVACY SERVICE INFORMATION

Privacy at GameLevel PT

GameLevel PT requests public League of Legends match information for the Riot ID you submit. We use that information to calculate the analysis and generate a training routine.

What is stored

Routine completion and growth history are stored in your current browser using local storage. Clearing browser data removes that history. A short-lived server cache may be used to reduce repeated Riot API requests.

What we do not request

GameLevel PT does not request your Riot password, payment information, or access to your Riot account.

Third-party data

Match information is supplied through Riot Games services and remains subject to Riot Games policies.
